THE GUARDIANS OF ESSENCE

The purpose

Culture remembered. Knowledge made useful.

uNdoqo documents and interprets Indigenous histories, languages, identities and built heritage, connecting rigorous research with accessible public learning.

Through Sandile Tukani University, we share educational programmes, books, advisory services and public media rooted in this work.
