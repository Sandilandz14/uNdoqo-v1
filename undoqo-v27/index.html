THE GUARDIANS
OF ESSENCE

We are an indigenous sanctuary dedicated to the restoration of our essence and the indigenous indominable spirit. Our work is the "uNdoqo" - the core and essence of who we are.

By configuring indigenous onomastics, exploring traditional lineage frameworks, and restoring pre-colonial language matrices, Sandile Tukani University works with authentic knwoledge and uses this to build a modern future with deep roots.

OUR CORE PROJECTS

Project ||Ei!nasi

Structured course and deep-dive project for the advancement of indigenous knowledge systems, deciphering roots and mapping click phonemes across Khwe-!Kwi-Tuu-Ntu languages. Discover the untapped hidden history of our people, the rich heritage hidden in our clan names and oral lores.

Explore Course

!Kora Kobab-ada

Revive, restore and explore !Korakobab, the story of the !Korana people and their connections with the rest of South African societies.

KwaXhosa Zibenza Zibutya
